# RemeMate

## 中文

RemeMate 想帮助语言学习者把真实遇到的词和句子留下来、记住，并真正用出去，而不是只学习一套通用词表。

你可以用 RemeMate：

- 从手动输入、文本、CSV 和 PDF 阅读中收集词汇与原句；
- 通过间隔复习巩固词汇，并用 AI 生成短故事、辅助造句和批改；
- 使用 SessionPad 记录语言交换、发送反馈，并把伙伴提供的表达整理成自己的学习内容；
- 在中文或英文界面中管理多种学习语言。

RemeMate 使用并感谢这些开源项目与开放资源：[Flask](https://flask.palletsprojects.com/)、[SQLAlchemy](https://www.sqlalchemy.org/)、[HTMX](https://htmx.org/)、[PostgreSQL](https://www.postgresql.org/)、[pypdf](https://github.com/py-pdf/pypdf)、[Lute v3](https://github.com/LuteOrg/lute-v3) 带来的阅读学习启发，以及 Wiktionary/Kaikki 和 JMdict 等开放词典资源。特别感谢 [WordNest](https://github.com/wink-wink-wink555/WordNest) 项目及其作者 [@wink-wink-wink555](https://github.com/wink-wink-wink555)。

## English

RemeMate helps language learners capture words and sentences from real life, remember them, and use them—rather than relying only on generic vocabulary lists.

With RemeMate, you can:

- collect vocabulary and source sentences from manual input, text, CSV files, and PDF reading;
- review with spaced repetition, then use AI-assisted short stories, writing practice, and corrections;
- use SessionPad to record language exchanges, send feedback, and turn a partner’s expressions into personal learning material;
- manage multiple learning languages through a Chinese or English interface.

RemeMate is built with and grateful to open-source projects and open resources including [Flask](https://flask.palletsprojects.com/), [SQLAlchemy](https://www.sqlalchemy.org/), [HTMX](https://htmx.org/), [PostgreSQL](https://www.postgresql.org/), [pypdf](https://github.com/py-pdf/pypdf), the reading-learning inspiration of [Lute v3](https://github.com/LuteOrg/lute-v3), and open dictionary resources such as Wiktionary/Kaikki and JMdict. Special thanks to [WordNest](https://github.com/wink-wink-wink555/WordNest) and its author, [@wink-wink-wink555](https://github.com/wink-wink-wink555).
